Dual language immersion and the PPS rightsizing scenarios

What Scenarios A and B (released October 2026) would change for immersion families

PPS proposes to close or consolidate schools in two scenarios released for the board on October 6, 2026. Both scenarios move most of the district’s Spanish dual language immersion programs into fewer schools, and move or add Mandarin and Vietnamese sites. This site puts numbers on what that means for immersion families, using PPS’s own enrollment and lottery reports.

9 → 4 elementary Spanish immersion sites, today → Scenario A or B

57% of Spanish kindergarten lottery applications went to programs that would move (2022-23 to 2026-27)

1,586 immersion students today in programs that move in Scenario A

52% → 36% of school-age Spanish speakers live within 1 mile of a K-5 Spanish site (estimate; the drop is 11–20 points allowing for survey error)

What’s here

  • Demand & scorecard: how many families apply to each immersion program, compared with the schools the programs would move to; then, for every language, the sites, students and pathways in each scenario.
  • Equity: the case for a different solution for immersion families. Program moves fall on schools that are about half historically underserved, while closures fall mostly on whiter, higher-income schools. Programs with long waitlists are among those moved, and past moves lost families.
  • Lent & César Chávez: the 2023 Bridger → Lent move and César Chávez, the plan’s new Spanish hub, from 2019 to PPS’s 2031 projections.
  • Sources & methods: where every number comes from and what it can’t tell you.
